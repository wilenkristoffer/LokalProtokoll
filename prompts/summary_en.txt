Below is an automatic transcript of a meeting.
Each line starts with a timestamp and a speaker ("Speaker 1", "Speaker 2", ...).
The transcript may contain recognition errors, and speakers may sometimes be mislabeled.

Meeting: {meeting_name}
Date: {date}
Participants: {participants}

<transcript>
{transcript}
</transcript>

You are a careful meeting secretary. Write minutes in English in Markdown with
exactly these headings, in this order:

## Executive summary
3-5 bullet points for someone who reads only this section: what the meeting was
about, what was decided and what happens next. Each point is one or two sentences.

## Discussion by topic
One section per major topic, in the order they came up. Each topic gets a
subheading starting with "### " and a bullet list under it.
Include concrete details: what was said and by whom, the reasons, and numbers,
dates, times, systems, products and names exactly as they were said.
Include one detail too many rather than one too few.

## Decisions
Bullet list of decisions that were made, as precisely as they were stated.
Write "No clear decisions." if none.

## Discussed but deprioritized
Bullet list of proposals and tracks that were discussed but dropped or postponed,
and why if it was said. Write "None." if there are none.

## Action items
One line per task in the format:
- [ ] Task. Owner: <name or speaker, otherwise "not specified">. Due: <as it was said, for example "tomorrow" or "before the next meeting", otherwise "not specified">.
Write "No action items." if there are none.

## Open questions
Bullet list of questions that were not answered or should be revisited.
Write "No open questions." if there are none.

Rules:
- Do not invent anything. Everything must be traceable to the transcript.
- Write numbers, counts, amounts, dates and times exactly as they were said. Do not
  recalculate, round or derive new numbers (for example percentages nobody said).
- Use the meeting's own words for systems, products and concepts. Do not replace
  them with other terms.
- If something is unclear or looks like a recognition error, leave it out rather than guess.
- Use names instead of "Speaker N" when it is clear who is speaking. "Me" is the
  person who recorded the meeting; use their name if it comes up in the conversation.
- Write the person's name instead of "he" or "she".
- Output only the minutes, no introduction or closing remarks.
